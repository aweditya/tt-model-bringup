  % ------------------- Introduction -------------------
  \begin{block}{Introduction}
    Tenstorrent's \textbf{Blackhole P150} is a new ML accelerator
    (32~GB GDDR6, 110 Tensix cores, 1.39~MiB L1/core, 404~GB/s measured
    DRAM streaming). We brought up three modern LLMs on a $1{\times}4$
    P150 mesh:
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Qwen3.6-27B} --- dense GQA, 4-way tensor-parallel.
      \item \textbf{Qwen3.6-35B-A3B} --- MoE with Gated DeltaNet
            recurrence ($\sim$3~GB active params per token).
      \item \textbf{Gemma 4 12B} --- hybrid sliding/global attention,
            dual head\_dim (256/512), $v\_norm$ + per-layer scalar.
    \end{itemize}
    The first bringup (27B) took weeks; the third (Gemma 4 12B) walked
    from HF spec to HTTP chat in \textbf{36 hours}. The speedup is
    methodological --- a forking recipe on stable scaffolding, not
    cleverer kernels.
  \end{block}

  % ------------------- The Bringup Workflow -------------------
  \begin{block}{The Bringup Workflow}
    Every model walks the same staging ladder. Each rung gates on
    cosine vs.\ an HF oracle, not on ``it produces text''.

    \begin{center}\small
    \begin{tabular}{l l l}
      \toprule
      \textbf{Stage} & \textbf{Adds} & \textbf{Gate} \\
      \midrule
      v0.0 & HF CPU oracle (fp32, per-sub-op hooks)        & dump exists \\
      v0.1 & L0 only on $(1,4)$ mesh                      & $\cos \ge 0.999$ \\
      v0.2 & all $N$ layers + lm\_head                     & argmax @ pos 0 \\
      v0.3 & paged KV + multi-step decode                  & argmax 0..7 \\
      v0.3.3 & long context, needle haystack               & top1 $\ge 90\%$ \\
      v0.4 & trace capture (two-phase warmup)              & 100/100 traced == eager \\
      v1   & continuous batching $B{=}4$                   & 3a/3b/3c gates \\
      v2   & HTTP wire-up                                  & \code{curl /chat} works \\
      \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
    \end{center}

    \textbf{HF oracle, not a sibling TT path.} Per-sub-op forward hooks
    dump \code{.npy}; every TT change is gated against ground truth.
    Comparing TT-against-TT conflates ``bug'' with ``different and
    better.''

    \textbf{Dev harness amortizes the bootstrap.} A long-lived
    tmux'd Python process holds the model resident
    ($\sim$80~s for Gemma~4, $\sim$14~min for 35B); file-trigger probes
    \code{importlib.reload} per fire. Iteration drops from one full
    bootstrap to $\sim$30~s.

    \textbf{REUSE mandate.} Every new file cites the prior file it
    forks in its commit message. After three bringups, $\sim$80--90\%
    of each new model's CB module, dev harness, probes, and oracle is
    copied. Bug catalog is finite ($\sim$12 footguns); fixes are
    recognized in seconds.

    \textbf{Two-phase trace warmup.} Compile all paths first
    (\code{enable_trace=False}), synchronize, then capture all traces
    back-to-back. Otherwise decode JIT between captures corrupts
    prefill trace memory $\to$ 99\% CPU hang
    (tenstorrent/vllm\#352).
  \end{block}

  % ------------------- Validation Hierarchy -------------------
  \begin{block}{Validation Hierarchy}
    Six of seven silent-failure classes pass at decode pos 0. The only
    gate that catches all of them is the
    \textbf{teacher-forced multi-step cosine + MAD ladder} against an
    HF oracle.

    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Teacher-forced cosine ladder} --- per-position
            per-layer cos + MAD. Caught Gemma 4's
            $\texttt{SDPA scale}{=}1.0$ (invisible at pos 0; cos drops
            $0.99998 \to 0.66$ at pos 1) and Qwen3.6's
            $y = x/\mathrm{rms}(x) \cdot (1+\gamma)$ zero-centered
            RMSNorm (a two-character fix lifted top-1
            $82/97 \to 95/97$, median cos $0.95 \to 0.997$).
      \item \textbf{Needle haystack} --- random 8-char password at
            controlled depth. Passed Gemma 4 at $L{=}100/256/512$
            (3/3 verbatim retrieval).
      \item \textbf{MAD, not just cosine.} $\cos(a, k{\cdot}a) = 1$
            for any $k$; missing Gemma 4's $\texttt{layer\_scalar}$
            passed at $\cos{=}0.999975$ with MAD $3.64{\times}10^{2}$.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{block}

  % ------------------- Correctness Stack -------------------
  \begin{block}{Correctness Stack}
    Three orthogonal gates, run per model on every change.

    \begin{center}\small
    \begin{tabular}{l c c c}
      \toprule
      \textbf{Gate} & \textbf{Qwen3.6-27B} & \textbf{35B-A3B} & \textbf{Gemma 4 12B} \\
      \midrule
      Cos ladder (pos $\le 7$)       & PASS  & 7/8 PASS (cos$_{L32}{\ge}0.987$) & PASS \\
      Argmax match @ pos 0           & PASS  & PASS               & PASS \\
      Multi-step token-for-token     & PASS  & 5/6 (pos 4 = bf16 tie) & PASS \\
      Needle haystack                & $L{=}500$ PASS & $\sim$50\% per trial (bf16) & 3/3 ($L{\le}512$) \\
      Multi-turn KV retention        & PASS  & 3/3 PASS           & PASS \\
      Traced == Eager (100 toks)     & PASS  & PASS               & 100/100 PASS \\
      \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
    \end{center}

    The 35B free-run needle is \emph{coherent failure, never gibberish}
    --- a fundamental bf16 non-determinism floor, not a bug.
  \end{block}

  % ------------------- Performance Optimizations -------------------
  \begin{block}{Performance Optimizations}
    \textbf{Rule of thumb:} kernel-time wins translate $1{:}1$ in
    trace; dispatch-only fusions land at $\sim$5--10\% of their
    isolated eager gain. Predict trace ROI by asking ``kernel work or
    dispatch?''

    \begin{center}\footnotesize
    \begin{tabular}{l l r l}
      \toprule
      \textbf{Optimization} & \textbf{Model} & \textbf{$\Delta$ tok/s} & \textbf{Why} \\
      \midrule
      Paged SDPA + B3 (HiFi2)        & 27B TP   & \textbf{+62\%}       & kernel: O(MAX\_POS) $\to$ O(1) \\
      Vocab-sharded lm\_head         & Gemma 4  & \textbf{+8\%}        & 248k/262k vocab shards $4{\times}$ \\
      Vocab-sharded lm\_head         & 27B TP   & +5.1\%               & on-device argmax: 600KB $\to$ 8B \\
      A004 \code{core_grid=10x11}    & 35B-A3B  & $-30$ ms/tok         & ttnn auto-grid used 11/110 cores \\
      A008 bf8 MoE expert weights    & 35B-A3B  & $-29$ ms/tok         & halves DRAM streaming \\
      Owned fused decay/gate kernel  & 27B TP   & +2.5\%               & 10 ops $\to$ 1 (custom kernel) \\
      \code{num_links=2} all\_reduce & 27B TP   & +1.65\%              & 2 links/axis on P150x4 \\
      A002 fused Q/K L2-norm         & 35B-A3B  & $-1.13$ ms/tok       & dispatch fusion only \\
      \midrule
      DRAM-sharded MLP               & 27B      & \textbf{$2.1\times$ slower} & Galaxy-70B recipe; wrong scale \\
      Async \code{all_reduce_async}  & 27B TP   & $+4\%$ overhead      & no parallel CCL to overlap \\
      fp32 KV cache                  & 27B      & dead end             & paged SDPA rejects fp32 \\
      bf8 KV cache                   & 27B      & neutral              & not memory-bound at $\le$8k \\
      \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
    \end{center}

    The 35B perf session stacked A002+A003+A004+A008 to take
    \textbf{141.79 $\to$ 81.16 ms/tok} (1.75$\times$) in one day. The
    big lever is always kernel-time fusion or smarter weight
    streaming; trace already amortizes dispatch.
  \end{block}

  % ------------------- Throughput Table -------------------
  \begin{block}{Throughput \& Roofline}
    Decode at $B{=}1$ is weight-bandwidth-bound: ceiling
    $\approx \tfrac{\text{active GB/chip}}{404\,\text{GB/s}}$.

    \begin{center}\small
    \begin{tabular}{l r r r r}
      \toprule
      \textbf{Model} & \textbf{Eager ms/tok} & \textbf{Traced ms/tok} & \textbf{tok/s} & \textbf{\% of ceiling} \\
      \midrule
      Qwen3.6-27B (TP)    & $\sim$253 & \textbf{77.0}  & \textbf{12.93} & $12.93/60 = $ \textbf{22\%} \\
      Qwen3.6-35B-A3B     & 480       & \textbf{81.16} & \textbf{12.32} & $12.32/270 = $ \textbf{4.6\%} \\
      Gemma 4 12B         & 182.7     & \textbf{47.5}  & \textbf{21.05} & $21.05/67.3 = $ \textbf{31\%} \\
      \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
    \end{center}

    \textbf{Continuous batching --- the largest single lever.}
    Matmul weight reads amortize across $B$. HTTP stress
    ($B{=}32$ slots, paged SDPA, per-slot KV):

    \begin{center}\small
    \begin{tabular}{r r r r r}
      \toprule
      \textbf{Clients} & \textbf{27B tok/s} & \textbf{vs 1c} & \textbf{Gemma 4 IT tok/s} & \textbf{vs 1c} \\
      \midrule
      1   & 5.36              & $1.00\times$       & 5.29              & $1.00\times$ \\
      8   & 40.87             & $7.63\times$       & 41.80             & $7.90\times$ \\
      16  & 79.59             & $14.85\times$      & 83.91             & $15.86\times$ \\
      32  & \textbf{156.59}   & $156.59/5.36 = $ \textbf{29.2$\times$} & \textbf{162.85} & $162.85/5.29 = $ \textbf{30.8$\times$} \\
      \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
    \end{center}

    Both models hit $\sim$160 tok/s aggregate at 32 concurrent clients
    with near-perfectly-linear $29$--$31{\times}$ scaling from one
    client, validating the CB design.

    \textbf{Multi-turn prefix cache (27B).} Turn 2 = $9.03$~s
    (172-tok prompt) vs.\ turn 1's $19.26$~s (105 tok):
    \textbf{6.3$\times$ speedup} on PC-hit turn. Gemma 4 PC still
    misses on chat-template re-renders (open bug).

  \end{block}

  % ------------------- Challenges -------------------
  \begin{block}{Challenges}
    Six bug classes; each paid for in chip-hours and now in
    \code{research/model_bringup_recipe.md}.

    \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{0.3ex}
      \item \textbf{ttnn view-decay.} \code{ttnn.slice} and
            \code{ttnn.reshape} return \emph{views}; deallocating the
            source corrupts reads. Masked at pos 0 because
            $\mathrm{softmax}(1)=1$ so attn\_out $=$ V.
            \emph{Resolution:} never add \code{deallocate} calls the
            upstream pattern didn't have.
      \item \textbf{Model-family semantic surprises.} Gemma 4 SDPA
            uses $\texttt{scale}{=}1.0$; Qwen3.6 RMSNorm is
            $(1+\gamma)$ zero-centered; Gemma 4 attention has
            \emph{three} per-head norms ($q$, $k$, $v\_norm$) plus a
            per-layer \code{layer_scalar}. All invisible at pos 0.
            \emph{Resolution:} multi-step teacher-forced + read HF.
      \item \textbf{bf16 chain non-determinism at $B{>}1$.} Every
            primitive bit-identical; 40-layer chain compounds
            $\sim$1 ULP into argmax flips. \emph{Resolution:} gate on
            per-slot independence, not exact tokens.
      \item \textbf{Silent perf regression --- caught by audit
            subagent.} CB engine silently routed 27B through
            \code{manual} DN recurrence; first ``fix'' targeted
            \code{deltanet_*} (single-stream gate only). CB forward
            actually reads \code{cb_dn_recurrence_mode} which
            defaulted to \code{manual} via \code{getattr}. Two-line
            fix took $B{=}4$ HTTP $13.30 \to B{=}32 = 156.59$ tok/s.
            \emph{Resolution:} audit subagent citing file:line.
      \item \textbf{Dev-harness PASS $\ne$ HTTP PASS.} 4/4 PASS in
            harness, first \code{/v1/chat/completions} crashed.
            \emph{Resolution:} \code{curl} smoke every backend swap.
      \item \textbf{Chip changes overnight.} 35B drift cliff
            reproducible for days, vanished with no code change. P150
            firmware v19.5.0 silently disabled 20 cores
            ($140 \to 120$). \emph{Resolution:} post-firmware
            regression runs.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{block}

  % ------------------- Tenstorrent vs DIY Split -------------------
  \begin{block}{What We Forked vs What We Built}
    \begin{center}\small
    \begin{tabular}{l r l}
      \toprule
      \textbf{Surface} & \textbf{Count} & \textbf{Origin} \\
      \midrule
      \code{ttnn.*} (SDPA, CCL, RMSNorm)   & $\sim$1,570 calls & tt-metal \\
      \code{ttnn.experimental.qwen36_*}    & 7 ops             & ours (owned) \\
      \code{owned_ops/} C++/HPP            & 8,116 LOC         & ours (Gated DN) \\
      \code{serve/server_*.py}             & 16,206 LOC        & wiring + HTTP \\
      \code{cb/} (scheduler + harness)     & 11,383 LOC        & ours (CB) \\
      \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
    \end{center}

    \textbf{$\sim$225:1 TT:ours ratio, but the few originals gated
    correctness for each new model.} Tenstorrent's openness is the
    only reason a one-person project shipped three LLM families in a
    quarter.
  \end{block}

  % ------------------- Future Work / Cleanup -------------------
  \begin{block}{Future Work}
    From the code-cleanup audit (18 items, 6 High).
    \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{0.3ex}
      \item \textbf{Argmax-tail trace in HTTP} --- closes the
            $156 \to 376$ tok/s gap on 27B $B{=}32$ (\code{cb_api.py}
            forces \code{sampling=True}).
      \item \textbf{Gemma 4 chat-template for prefix cache} ---
            re-renders don't byte-equal; port Qwen3.6's patches for
            $\sim$6$\times$ multi-turn speedup.
      \item \textbf{Gemma 4 P2/P3 distributed RMSNorm + paged SDPA
            globals} --- projected $+30$--$60\%$; closes the
            $31\% \to 60\%$ roofline gap.
      \item \textbf{35B $B{>}1$ empty-slot poisoning} (\#162) ---
            unlocks 35B CB beyond \code{TT_CB_SLOTS=1}.
      \item \textbf{Delete dead manual DN path} --- $\sim$1.5K LOC;
            structurally broken (cos $0.08$).
    \end{itemize}
  \end{block}

  % ------------------- Conclusions -------------------
  \begin{block}{Conclusions}
    Three bringups, each faster (27B $\sim$3 wks, 35B $\sim$2 wks,
    Gemma 4 \textbf{36 hrs}). Compounding from \emph{methodology}:
    staging ladder + HF oracle + dev harness + REUSE. CB delivers
    \textbf{$156$/$163$~tok/s on 27B/Gemma 4} at $B{=}32$.
  \end{block}
